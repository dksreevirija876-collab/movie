Our Origin

Redefining how the world discovers and experiences cinema.

Founded in 2022 by a passionate group of filmmakers, critics, and tech innovators, CineStream was created to bridge the divide between blockbuster sensations and underrepresented independent cinema.

We set out to engineer a streaming architecture that delivers lossless audio, crystal-clear 4K HDR playback, and an intelligent recommendation engine crafted around artistic sensibilities rather than mindless algorithms.
